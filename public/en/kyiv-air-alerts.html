Air raid alerts in Kyiv: history and statistics

Air Alert Stat keeps a history of air raid alerts in Kyiv since 25 February 2022: how many alerts there were in a day, month or year, how much time the city spent under alert and at what time of day alerts are most common.

Quick answers

  • How many air raid alerts has Kyiv had since the full-scale invasion? Current totals, records and yearly tables are on the Kyiv alert statistics page.
  • How many alerts were there this month? The monthly archive has a page for every month with a day-by-day table and a comparison with the previous month.
  • How long was the longest alert? Records such as the longest continuous alert and the most alerts in one day are calculated automatically from the whole archive.
  • Where can I get the raw data? Daily values can be downloaded as open CSV data.

Interactive dashboard

The dashboard shows daily statistics, a 24-hour timeline of alert intervals and trends over equal periods. The map shows aggregated reports of attack consequences by Kyiv administrative district. Select a district to see the researched records linked to it and their sources.

The last seven days are shown by default. You can choose 3, 7, 30, 90 or 180 days or a custom calendar range, and share the selected view from the address bar.

An air raid alert is not the same as an attack

Alert intervals come from warning systems. Information about damage, casualties or locations is collected separately from publications and documented with a link to the source. One alert does not mean one strike, and one strike does not necessarily correspond to a separate alert.

How to read the charts

The count is the number of alert intervals that started on a given day. Alert time adds up the duration of alerts and is split at midnight Kyiv time. Alerts in Kyiv are declared for the whole city. A day without a stored interval is not proof that no alert happened.

District map and safety

Aggregated markers represent a district, not a point of impact. Recent exact strike locations, critical infrastructure and air-defence positions are not published. To check a record, open the sources in its incident card.
