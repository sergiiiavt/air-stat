Air raid alert statistics for Kyiv and Kyiv Oblast

Air Alert Stat is an interactive history of air raid alerts in Kyiv and Kyiv Oblast and of researched reports of attack consequences. Choose a period, explore the district map, daily and 24-hour timelines and trends.

Open the dashboard

Statistics archive

  • Kyiv: alerts by year and month since 2022 — totals, records and a page for every month.
  • Kyiv Oblast: alerts by year and month since 2022.
  • Open data (CSV) with daily counts and durations.

What the dashboard shows

  • Map — researched reports of attack consequences aggregated by Kyiv district or Kyiv Oblast community, with sources for every record.
  • By day — the number of alerts and total alert time for each day of the selected period.
  • 24 hours — when exactly alerts started and ended during each day, in Kyiv time.
  • Trends — equal periods compared side by side, so weeks or months can be compared fairly.
  • Ballistics — episodes of ballistic missile attacks identified from sources.

The map can show Kyiv City, Kyiv Oblast or both; the other views cover Kyiv City. Every view works with a preset or custom period, and the address bar keeps the selection so it can be shared. Monthly and yearly figures for both areas are in the statistics archive.

Important

Alert statistics and news reports about consequences are separate datasets. A missing record does not prove that nothing happened. Exact coordinates of recent strikes are not published. This is historical analytics, not a warning service: during an alert, follow official signals.
