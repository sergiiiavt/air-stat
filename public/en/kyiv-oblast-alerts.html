Air raid alerts in Kyiv Oblast

The Kyiv Oblast alert archive starts on 15 March 2022. Separately, the map lets you explore reports of attack consequences by town, district and community.

Kyiv Oblast air raid alert statistics by year and month

What “an alert in the oblast” means

Alerts in the oblast are declared for the whole oblast, for individual districts or for communities. Air Alert Stat merges these intervals: alert time is the time when an alert was active in at least part of the oblast. That is why oblast alert time is usually longer than Kyiv’s. Kyiv City is not included in these figures.

Finding a place

On the dashboard choose the “Oblast” or “Both” scope and a period. Clicking an aggregated marker or a territory name filters researched incidents to that territory only. The oblast covers settlements around Kyiv; its records are never replaced by records from the city.

What a marker means

The number on a marker is the count of researched incidents assigned to an administrative territory in the selected period. It is not a number of missiles or an estimate of combat intensity. When a source does not give precise enough geography, the record can still appear in the totals without a map point.

Sources and limits

  • Kyiv City: Kyiv Digital open data from 25 February 2022; alerts.in.ua from 16 September 2026 as an additional source.
  • Kyiv Oblast: the national air-alert channel via the open ukrainian-air-raid-sirens-dataset from 15 March 2022 to 16 May 2026; the Kyiv Oblast Military Administration Telegram channel from 16 May 2026; alerts.in.ua from 16 September 2026.

Researched attack consequences are a separate dataset and not a complete register of all events. Open the primary sources and check the verification labels. Public points can be generalized for safety; they are not exact impact addresses.
