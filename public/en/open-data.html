Open data on air raid alerts in Kyiv and Kyiv Oblast

The daily values behind the dashboard and the monthly archive are available for download. Files are generated automatically from the current database and refreshed at least every few hours.

Files

  • kyiv-city-daily.csv — Kyiv City, from 25 February 2022.
  • kyiv-oblast-daily.csv — Kyiv Oblast (excluding Kyiv City), from 15 March 2022.

Fields

FieldMeaning
dateCalendar date in Kyiv time (YYYY-MM-DD)
alert_countNumber of alert intervals that started on the day (overlaps counted once)
alert_secondsSeconds of alert time within the day; overnight alerts are split at midnight
alert_hoursThe same in hours, two decimal places

UTF-8, comma-separated, with a header row. The last row is the current day and still changes.

Sources

  • Kyiv City: Kyiv Digital open data from 25 February 2022; alerts.in.ua from 16 September 2026 as an additional source.
  • Kyiv Oblast: the national air-alert channel via the open ukrainian-air-raid-sirens-dataset from 15 March 2022 to 16 May 2026; the Kyiv Oblast Military Administration Telegram channel from 16 May 2026; alerts.in.ua from 16 September 2026.

The calculation is described in the methodology. A day without an interval is not proof that no alert happened. Check the terms of the primary sources before republishing.

How to cite

Air Alert Stat, “Open data on air raid alerts in Kyiv and Kyiv Oblast”, https://air-alert-stat.com/en/open-data, date of download. The calculation code is open in the project repository.

Historical analytics, not a live warning service. Data can be incomplete; during an alert follow official signals.
